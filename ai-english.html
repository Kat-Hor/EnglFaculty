5 Golden Rules ⭐

1

Use approved tools

Only use AI tools your school allows. Check age limits — many chatbots require users to be 13+ or need parental consent.

2

No personal data

No names, photos, addresses or grades in a prompt. Ever.

3

Be transparent

Tell students clearly when AI is allowed — and ask them to document how they used it (chat log or short note).

4

Assess the thinking

Grade what students do with the AI: checking, improving, arguing, reflecting — not the AI's text.

5

AI makes mistakes

Use that! Spotting errors, bias and invented facts is a fantastic learning task.

The AI Traffic Light 🚦

Green

AI is allowed and part of the task. Students document their prompts and results.

Amber

AI only for specific steps (e.g. feedback or practice) — the final product is the student's own work.

Red

No AI. Typical for tests, exams and the live performance after an AI practice phase.
