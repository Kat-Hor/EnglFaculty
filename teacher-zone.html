How to assess AI tasks 🧭

🧠

Assess the thinking

Grade what students do with the AI: checking, improving, arguing, reflecting — never the AI's text itself.

🔁

Process counts

Ask for drafts, chat logs and the "AI note". Revision and reflection can carry points.

🔴

RED for the product

Practise with AI (GREEN/YELLOW), but let students write or perform the graded part on their own.

📏

Transparent criteria

Show students the rubric before they start. Adapt weighting to your course and the curriculum.
