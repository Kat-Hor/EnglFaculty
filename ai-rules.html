AI Rules for Students 🚦

AI can be a super helper — if you use it the right way. Before every task, your teacher shows you the AI traffic light. It tells you if and how you may use AI. Let's learn the rules!

STOP!

The AI Traffic Light

6 Golden Rules ⭐

1🧠

Brain first!

Think first, then prompt. Collect your own ideas before you ask the AI. You want to learn — not the computer.

2🔒

Keep it private

Never type your name, address, photos, passwords or information about other people into an AI.

3✅

Only allowed tools

Use only the AI tools your school and your teacher allow — and only when the traffic light says so.

4🔍

Check everything

AI makes mistakes and sometimes invents facts. Check important information in a reliable source.

5🙋

Be honest

Always say when and how you used AI. Use the "My AI note" box below. Secret AI use is cheating.

6✍️

Make it yours

AI is a helper, not a ghostwriter. Your text, your words, your ideas — the AI can only give tips.

My AI Note 📝

Write this under your work every time you used AI:
  1. I used   (name of the AI tool).
  2. I used it for   (e.g. feedback, ideas, checking grammar).
  3. My most important prompt was:  
  4. I changed / didn't use   because  .
